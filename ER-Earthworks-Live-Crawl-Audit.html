The site has real strengths: strong keyword coverage, 16 location-specific suburb pages, a clear service offering, and good brand messaging. But there are several embarrassing live issues that undermine credibility — most critically, every image slider still has the default placeholder text "Slide title / Write your caption here" visible to every visitor and search engine. These are ordered by fastest impact with least effort first.

Top 10 Recommendations — Crawled June 2026

1
Fix all slider captions — remove "Slide title / Write your caption here" Critical Quick Win High Impact
Every image slider on the homepage has the platform's default placeholder text live and visible. This is the most urgent fix on the site.
+
There are at least 13 slider images with "Slide title" as the heading and "Write your caption here" as the caption. This text is visible to every visitor and is being indexed by Google as page content. It signals an unfinished, amateur website to both visitors and search engines. The images themselves have good alt text keywords (excavation Gold Coast, bobcat hire Gold Coast etc) which is positive — the captions just need to be replaced or removed entirely.
Found live on the site Slide title / Write your caption here — repeated 13+ times across two separate sliders on the homepage

How to fix it

  1. Log into the Duda website editor
  2. Click on each slider element on the homepage
  3. Either delete the caption text entirely (cleanest option) or replace with a real caption
  4. Repeat for both sliders — there are two separate ones on the homepage
  5. Publish changes
Option A — delete captions entirely (recommended)
Remove all heading and caption text from sliders. Let the images speak for themselves. The alt text already has the keywords.
Option B — replace with real captions
Slide 1: "Excavation & Site Preparation — Gold Coast" Slide 2: "Bobcat & Skid Steer Hire — Residential & Commercial" Slide 3: "Tip Truck Hire — Soil Removal & Cartage" (continue in same format for remaining slides)

4
Add internal links between equipment pages and location pages SEO Short Session High Impact
No page on the site links to any other page within the body copy. Navigation links only. This is a significant structural SEO gap.
+
Every page — homepage, equipment page, and all 16 location pages — relies entirely on navigation links for internal linking. There are no contextual links within body copy. When the Southport page mentions "excavators" it doesn't link to the equipment page. When the equipment page mentions service areas it doesn't link to the location pages. This means Google can't understand the relationship between pages and which ones are most important. It also means users have no natural path through the site.
Confirmed across all crawled pages Homepage, /our-equipment, and /earthmoving-hire-southport all contain zero contextual body copy links to other pages

How to fix it

  1. On the homepage, link "excavators" to /our-equipment, link "Gold Coast" service area mentions to top location pages
  2. On /our-equipment, add a "We service all Gold Coast suburbs" line with links to 4-5 key location pages
  3. On each location page, link equipment mentions (excavator, bobcat, tip truck) to /our-equipment
  4. Every page should have a clear text link to /contact-us in the body copy, not just the nav
Example internal link to add to each location page
We offer a full range of [excavation equipment for hire](/our-equipment) including excavators from 1 to 20 tonne, bobcats, and tip trucks. [Get in touch](/contact-us) for availability and pricing.

The homepage slider images all have descriptive alt text (excavation Gold Coast, bobcat hire Gold Coast etc) which is genuinely good. But the equipment icons, service images, and gallery images in the lower sections of the page appear with empty image references. These are invisible to Google and screen readers. How to fix it

  1. In the Duda editor, click each image in the equipment and gallery sections

  3. Use the format: [what is shown] + [location/context]

Alt text formula

Increase readable text content — site is image-heavy and text-light Content GEO Half Day High Impact
The homepage has hundreds of images but relatively little indexable text. Google and AI tools need readable text to understand and rank the page. The site scores 4/10 on content depth.
+
The homepage is dominated by two large image sliders, a 30+ image gallery grid, and icon sections with minimal copy. The actual indexable text is thin — a few paragraphs and some bullet points. Google crawls text, not images. AI tools cite text, not images. Adding one well-written section that answers common customer questions — "What size excavator do I need?", "How much does excavator hire cost on the Gold Coast?" — would dramatically improve both Google rankings and AI visibility.

  4. Each addition should be 50-100 words — enough for Google to index, not so long it clutters the page
