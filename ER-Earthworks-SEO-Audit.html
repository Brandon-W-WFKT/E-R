High Priority
Page Title

Title is decent but "Best" is a red flag word

Current title: "E&R Earthworks Gold Coast – Best Excavator Hire Gold Coast". The word "Best" is a superlative claim that Google treats with scepticism, and it's overused in low-quality SEO. It also duplicates "Gold Coast" twice. Consider a cleaner version:

Suggested new title
E&R Earthworks | Excavator & Earthmoving Hire Gold Coast | Since 1998

The "Since 1998" angle is a genuine differentiator and trust signal that competitors can't fake.

Duda Editor > Page Settings > SEO

Suburb Location Pages

erearthworks.com.au/earthmoving-hire-[suburb]
Critical
Smart Strategy — Poorly Executed

16 suburb pages in the footer — this is the right idea but likely thin content

Having dedicated suburb pages (Robina, Burleigh, Coomera, Helensvale etc) is a strong local SEO strategy. However, these pages are almost certainly thin — likely just the homepage content with the suburb name swapped in. If Google detects duplicate or near-duplicate content across 16 pages, it may penalise or ignore them entirely.

⚠️ This is a double-edged sword. Done well, suburb pages drive serious local traffic. Done poorly (thin, duplicate content), they can actively hurt rankings. These pages need a proper audit before proceeding.

Each suburb page needs at minimum: a unique opening paragraph specific to that suburb, at least 300 words of unique content, and a local reference (e.g. "We work regularly across Coomera, including Hope Island Road and the newer residential estates near Dreamworld").

Priority audit — check each suburb page individually

Technical & Structural Issues

High Priority
Platform

Built on Duda — not Wix

This site is built on Duda, not Wix. The SEO panel, meta settings, and image management all work differently. The Duda platform has its own SEO tools under Site > SEO in the dashboard. The recommendations in this report all apply but the "where to find it" steps differ from the Wix checklist.

Schema / Structured Data

No visible structured data for a local business

There's no LocalBusiness schema markup visible on the site. This structured data tells Google the business name, address, phone, hours, and service area in a standardised format that directly feeds into Google's knowledge graph and local search results. Duda supports adding schema through custom code injection.

Key schema fields to implement
Business name: E&R Earthworks
Address: [full street address]
Phone: 07 5522 9825
Service area: Gold Coast, Brisbane, Northern NSW
Business type: ExcavationContractor
Founded: 1998
Duda > Site Settings > Custom Code or Schema plugin
Google Business Profile

Verify GBP is set up, verified, and complete

The Google Maps link in the footer shows a GBP listing exists for E&R Earthworks. Confirm it's verified, has complete information, recent photos, and is actively accumulating reviews. The "Our Customers Reviews" section on the site doesn't show any actual reviews — which likely means they're pulling from a widget that may not be configured.

The reviews section heading appears twice on both the homepage and services page but shows no actual review content. Either the Google Reviews widget isn't connected or it's not rendering. Fix this — social proof is critical for a trade business.
business.google.com + Duda widget settings
Content Structure

Pages are image-heavy and text-light — Google has little to index

Both the homepage and services page are primarily image sliders and icon grids. The actual readable text Google can index is minimal. A page with 500 words of well-structured content outranks a page with 50 words and 30 images every time. The content that is there (the "Why Choose E&R" section, the machinery descriptions) is good — there just needs to be more of it.

Specifically, the homepage needs a more substantial "About Us" section explaining the history, experience, and what makes E&R different in 200-300 words. This also helps with GEO visibility if they ever move to that.
