Standard Pool Digs

Tight-Access Excavation

Common Questions

Pool Dig FAQs

How long does a pool dig take?

Most residential pool digs on the Gold Coast take one to two days depending on size, shape, and ground conditions. If we hit rock, it can extend by a day. We'll give you a realistic timeline when we quote — not an optimistic one that blows out.

Do I need council approval before you dig?

Your pool builder will handle the pool approval with council. You should have your Development Approval (DA) or building approval in place before we start the dig. We don't require any separate approval for the excavation itself.

What if there's rock underground?

It's more common than people expect, particularly in the hinterland and escarpment areas. We carry hydraulic rock breakers on our excavators and can handle it on-site. We'll let you know if rock is a likely factor for your specific location when we quote.

Can you access a tight backyard or narrow side gate?

Yes. We carry mini excavators from 1 tonne that can fit through access as narrow as 900mm. If we need to remove a fence panel temporarily, we can coordinate that with you. We've done hundreds of tight-access digs across Gold Coast residential blocks.

Do you remove the excavated soil?

Yes, always. Our tipper trucks are included in the job. We load and remove all spoil from site so your yard is clean when we're done. If you want some of the topsoil kept for garden fill, just let us know when we're quoting.

Do you work with pool builders directly?

Absolutely. Most Gold Coast pool builders use us regularly and can deal with us directly to book the dig around their construction schedule. If you're a builder and want to set up an account, give us a call on 07 5522 9825.
