Block Preparation Specialists

The Job That Sets Up Everything Else

A site cut done right is the foundation for everything that follows — your slab, your footings, your driveway, your drainage. Get it wrong and you're fixing problems for the life of the build.

E&R Earthworks has been preparing Gold Coast blocks for residential and commercial construction since 1998. We know the terrain, we understand the soil conditions across the region, and we work to the tolerances your builder needs — not close enough, but right.

Whether it's a single house block in Coomera, a duplex site in Robina, or a commercial development in Ormeau, we bring the right equipment and the right operators for the job.

  • Residential site cuts for new builds, extensions and additions
  • Commercial block preparation for multi-lot and industrial sites
  • Land clearing including tree and stump removal
  • Cut and fill — balancing cut material before removing excess
  • Level pad preparation for sheds, garages and pools
  • Drainage shaping and fall preparation
  • Council-compliant retaining wall excavation
What We Do

Site Cut & Clearing Services

Every block is different. We scope the job properly before we quote so you get an accurate price — not a low number that blows out when we hit something unexpected.

01 —

Residential Site Cuts

New home construction, granny flats, extensions, and additions. We cut to the engineer's specified levels and hand over a site your builder can work straight onto. Includes spoil removal and tip truck hire.

02 —

Commercial Block Prep

Multi-lot residential, industrial sites, and commercial developments. Larger equipment, faster turnaround. We've worked alongside civil engineers and project managers across South East Queensland for over 30 years.

03 —

Land Clearing

Scrub, trees, stumps, and vegetation removal before building begins. We clear the block and remove all material from site. Council vegetation clearing permits may be required — check before you book.

04 —

Cut & Fill Balancing

On sloping blocks, a cut and fill approach reuses excavated material to level the site — reducing the volume you need to truck away. We assess the material suitability on site and work to your engineer's specs.

05 —

Drainage Preparation

Shaping the site to correct falls for stormwater drainage before slab or landscape works begin. Done now, it prevents drainage problems for the life of the build. Often done alongside the site cut.

06 —

Level Pads & Hardstand

Preparing flat, compacted pads for sheds, garages, pool surrounds, and hardstand areas. Includes any necessary fill supply and compaction to the required depth and bearing capacity.
